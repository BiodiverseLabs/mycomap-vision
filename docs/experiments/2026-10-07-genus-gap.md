## Question
What explains iNat's lead at genus on the 307-record comparison?

## Why it matters
If iNat's lead were method, our approach would need rethinking; if it is depth, more references
close it.

## Setup
Per-record comparison of Vision (sample fine-tune, nearest) and iNat CV (with location) at genus.

## What we tried
Breakdown by the true genus' reference records; a reading of every record only iNat got right.

## Results
Right at genus: Vision 191, iNat 218. Of the 56 only iNat got right: 27 had the right genus in
Vision's top 5 (ranking), 10 genera were absent from the reference set, 9 had 1-4 references,
10 were real confusions.

Genus top-1 by the true genus' reference records, Vision vs iNat: 0: 0% vs 56%; 1-4: 43 vs 57;
5-19: 56 vs 63; 20-99: 68 vs 77; 100+: 80.9 vs 80.9.

Label problem found: 9 green records named for a yeast that lives inside puffballs
(*Teunomyces*) made 4 *Pisolithus* test records come back as *Teunomyces*; the photos show the
host.

## Verdict
Depth, plus some guest-organism labels.

## Decision
Exclude guest genera (guest-genera); run on the full data (full-run-finetune).

Provisional: an exploratory result from before the dataset freeze; to be re-run on dataset release v1 (docs/PLAN.md, "a reproducible dataset release").
