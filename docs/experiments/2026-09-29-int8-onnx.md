## Question
Is dynamic 8-bit quantization of the image tower safe?

## Why it matters
The 4 GB CPU server answers about 13 identifications per minute; int8 would more than double
that.

## Setup
ONNX export (LayerNorm and GELU fused; attention not), dynamic int8 on query photos only;
reference vectors unchanged.

## What we tried
Frozen BioCLIP 2 on 2,000 sample photos (2026-09-29); the served fine-tuned model on the full
1,152-record comparison (2026-10-09).

## Results
Sample, species / genus / family top-1: int8 14.1 / 47.5 / 47.5 vs full precision 14.5 / 48.2 / 47.4;
the same answer for 74 / 81 / 85% of photos; median confidence change 0.025.

Full-run model, 1,152 records: species 33.9 vs 34.5, genus 69.4 vs 71.6 (-2.3), family 77.8 vs
79.7 (-1.9), first photo 26.9 vs 28.9. Laptop speed 249 vs 586 ms per photo. Cosine parity
0.966; ONNX at full precision reproduces the vectors exactly.

## Verdict
Not as-is.

## Decision
Keep full precision (Steve, 2026-10-09).

## Next
Revisit if traffic grows: a gentler int8 that keeps attention at full precision, or a faster
server.
